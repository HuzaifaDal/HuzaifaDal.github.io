About

I am a cybersecurity practitioner based in Pakistan with a background in offensive security and red teaming. My focus areas are offensive security, red teaming and vulnerability research, and right now I am actively focused on Active Directory and web application pentesting. I also hold a Red Team rank on WebVerse Labs.

I began my journey as an intern at Cyberster and at Developers Hub, and I am now a core team member at Cyberster, where I keep working on the offensive side of security.

On the credentials side I hold EC-Council C|PENT, Fortinet FortiGate 7.4 Operator, Fortinet Network Security Expert, Cisco Ethical Hacker and APIsec University credentials. Alongside the software side I also have a strong grip on industrial-level hardware, including hardware troubleshooting.
